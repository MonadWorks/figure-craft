# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making Figure Craft a welcoming, respectful, and useful project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Please:

- be respectful and constructive;
- discuss ideas and evidence rather than people;
- acknowledge uncertainty and different scientific or design contexts;
- give feedback that helps the work become clearer, more honest, or more useful;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and the boundaries of shared research material.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and knowingly misrepresenting data, sources, or contributions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

To report a concern, contact the project maintainer privately through the GitHub profile or repository contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Please include enough context to investigate; do not post sensitive information publicly.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
